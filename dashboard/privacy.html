Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This app ("Story Time Pipeline") is a personal automation tool that generates and uploads short-form videos to a single YouTube channel owned and operated by the developer.

What this app accesses

The app connects to exactly one YouTube channel — the developer's own — using the youtube.upload scope, solely to upload videos on that channel's behalf. It does not request or access data from any other Google or YouTube account.

What this app does not do

  • It does not collect, store, or share data belonging to any other user.
  • It does not sell, rent, or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It is not distributed to or used by anyone other than the developer.

This dashboard site

The analytics dashboard hosted alongside this page displays publicly available statistics (subscriber count, view count, video titles) for the same channel, fetched via the YouTube Data API. It does not use cookies, does not track visitors, and does not collect any information from people viewing it.
